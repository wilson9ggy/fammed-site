Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

FamMed is built around a simple principle: your health data never leaves your device.

Data stored locally

All of your data is stored locally on your device. We never upload your personal health information to any server.

No account required

No registration or personal information is required to use this app.

Notification permission only

We only request permission to send medication-reminder notifications. We do not access your contacts, location, or other sensitive information.

No data collection or tracking

This app does not collect any data for analytics or advertising purposes, and contains no third-party tracking SDKs.

Data export & deletion

You can export your data at any time; the exported file is saved only on your device. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Purchases

Subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed by Apple through your App Store account. We do not receive or store your payment information.

隐私政策

最近更新：2026 年 11 月 2 日

FamMed 遵循一个简单的原则：您的健康数据永不离开您的设备。

数据本地存储

您的所有数据都存储在设备本地，我们不会将您的个人健康信息上传到任何服务器。

无需账户

使用本应用无需注册账户或提供任何个人信息。

仅通知权限

我们仅请求发送服药提醒通知的权限，不会访问您的通讯录、位置或其他敏感信息。

不收集、不追踪

本应用不会收集任何用于分析或广告目的的数据，也不含任何第三方追踪 SDK。

数据导出与删除

您可以随时导出数据，导出文件仅保存在您的设备上。删除本应用即会从设备上清除其全部数据。

购买与订阅

订阅与 App 内购买由 Apple 通过您的 App Store 账户处理，我们不会接收或存储您的支付信息。
